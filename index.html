What we do

Embedded and Python engineering, under one roof.

We design the firmware that runs on the microcontroller and the PC-side automation, test and calibration tooling that drives it — as one connected engineering solution, not two separate hand-offs between teams.

01

Embedded Firmware Development

Bare-metal and RTOS firmware for ARM Cortex-M, precision analog and other industry-standard microcontroller families, from board bring-up and peripheral drivers to production-ready application code.

02

Device Drivers & Hardware Interfaces

Driver development for UART, SPI, I2C, ADC, QSPI and PWM, plus board support packages for hardware abstraction.

03

Python Automation & Engineering Tools

PyQt/PyQt5 desktop applications for calibration, test execution, configuration management and workflow automation.

04

IoT & Connectivity

MQTT and HTTP integration, cellular, Wi-Fi and GPS modules, and secure communication protocols that connect field devices to the cloud.

05

Real-Time Systems

FreeRTOS-based task scheduling, inter-task queues and interrupt-driven design for deterministic, multitasking embedded applications.

06

Testing, Validation & Signal Processing

Sensor characterization, calibration routines, digital filtering and structured test protocols that validate system behavior under real operating conditions.

About Haritron

We are engineers who build embedded products, connected systems and engineering software.

Founded in 2021 and based in Bangalore, Haritron works across embedded firmware, Python automation and IoT connectivity. Our project work spans precision sensor calibration systems, industrial machine control, health monitoring devices and connected tracking systems.

We treat the embedded side and the PC-side automation, test and calibration tooling as one connected engineering discipline — because in real products, they usually are.

Haritron was built with a dual purpose from day one: delivering dependable engineering work for commercial clients, while giving engineering students a place to learn firmware, automation and IoT development on real, working systems.

Engineering depth

Direct experience with device drivers, RTOS scheduling, signal processing and hardware-software integration.

Practical development

Systems built to work in the field — calibrated, tested and validated, not just prototyped.

Two disciplines, one team
